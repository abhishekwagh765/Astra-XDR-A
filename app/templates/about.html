About the Platform

Built for teams who run security
at Kubernetes speed.

ASTRA-XDR started as an answer to a simple frustration: cloud-native environments generate an overwhelming amount of security telemetry, and most of it never reaches the people who need it in time to act.

Our Mission

Close the gap between detection and response.

Modern infrastructure is ephemeral, distributed, and fast-moving — containers spin up and disappear in seconds, workloads scale across nodes, and attack surfaces shift constantly. Traditional, siloed security tooling simply wasn't designed for this.

ASTRA-XDR runs natively inside its own astra namespace, reading cluster state through a read-only service account, scoring pod health in real time, and surfacing Falco's runtime alerts the moment they're posted — all in one dashboard, not five.

What We Believe

Principles behind the platform

Visibility first

Cluster state, node/pod metrics, and Falco's runtime output are all pulled live — nothing is simulated or cached stale.

Least privilege, always

The astra-role ClusterRole is get/list/watch only. ASTRA-XDR observes your cluster; it never mutates it.

Security gates in CI, not after

SonarCloud and Trivy run before every deploy — a build with a critical vulnerability simply doesn't ship.
